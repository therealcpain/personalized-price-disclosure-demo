Legal disclaimer. This card is proposed-policy literacy — not legal advice, not a price scraper, not a VPN “beat the algorithm” tool, and not a claim that any named merchant violated Section 5. On Aug 19, 2026 the FTC proposed an Enforcement Policy Statement on personalized pricing: where consumers reasonably expect non-personalized prices, businesses “should clearly and conspicuously disclose” (1) that the price is personalized, (2) the basis for personalization, and (3) the types of data used. Calling a price “specially selected” alone is called likely misleading. Comment period extended Sep 3, 2026 — deadline September 25, 2026. This is a proposed statement, not a final rule. The FTC does not claim authority to ban personalized pricing outright. Sources: FTC Aug 19 2026 proposed statement (PDF) · FTC Sep 3 2026 press · Sep 25 deadline · FTC legal-library landing · regulations.gov · ReportFraud.ftc.gov. We never invent your “true” price or allege a Section 5 violation.
